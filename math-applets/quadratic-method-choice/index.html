Choose a Method to Solve a Quadratic

For each quadratic function, choose an efficient method to solve f(x) = 0. Use the clues in the function to decide which method fits best.

Example 1 of 12
Method reminders
  • Square Root: use when b = 0.
  • Factor: use when c = 0 or when a and c are perfect squares. A positive perfect-square discriminant also makes factoring an option; when a > 0, the quadratic formula is accepted too.
  • Complete the Square: use when a = 1 and b is even.
  • Quadratic Formula: use when a, b, and c are nonzero and the discriminant is not a perfect square, unless an earlier clue gives a more direct method.
  • Graph: this is not the most efficient method unless you have a graphing calculator or Desmos.
